Booking information

Please come with your hair washed and ready for service. Blow drying is included with your appointment.

A $20 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment and will be applied toward your final service total.

Must cancel 24 hours in advance to reschedule. Rescheduling within 24 hours of the appointment will result in a forfeited deposit.

Please bring your preferred braiding hair unless we discuss a different arrangement before your appointment.
